## Summary
Throughout my career, I have partnered with security and engineering teams to find what matters most in their environments and turn it into practical change. Most recently, as a Forward Deployed Engineer at Ghost Security, I built AI agents that do real security work for enterprise teams, with a focus on trust, transparency, safety, and leveling up my clients. Before that, I advised organizations on securing AWS, Google Cloud, and Kubernetes as a cloud security consultancy co-founder and a Google Cloud PSO consultant. My background also includes vuln research, pen-testing, security operations, and CTF/cyber range development.
